Adds copy, cut and move with insertion to Calc.

Select rows, columns or cells, right-click (or open the Edit menu), choose an action and click the destination.

Several rows (or columns) selected with Ctrl are inserted together, in sheet order.

Sheets: right-click a sheet tab (or select several tabs first), choose Copy sheets and insert > Before/After and click the tab of the destination sheet.
The sheets are copied as one block and the references between them follow the normal Calc paste rules.
All the selected sheets must be unprocted and consecutive (Shift+click on the tabs), and the copies must be inserted outside the range of the selected sheets: before the first one or after the last one.

Rows go above/below, columns left/right, cells in all four directions.
Cut and move keep formulas intact, Move empties the source cells but keeps their formatting, Undo reselects the source, a protected source sheet dims every menu entry and a protected destination sheet cancels the action with a message (except when copying sheets, where the destination only gives the position).

The menus follow the interface language (English or French for now).
